How the value is fitted — and tested

Every term earned its place out of sample. The table below is 4-fold cross-validation over the traded protocols: each protocol predicted once, from a model fitted without it. The blend wins on median error and rank correlation. Two terms were tested the same way and dropped: the seven-day growth term and a chain-count term did not improve out-of-sample error, so they are not in the model.

How the multiples are set

Each category's multiple is the median market-cap-to-TVL ratio of the protocols in it whose token already trades, shrunk toward the global median with a strength of 20 peers. Method

A protocol "records no token" when the snapshot carries neither a CoinGecko id nor a ticker for it. That is the snapshot's record, not proof it has none: a protocol on the board may still have a token its record does not carry. Implied values are this radar's own claim, never a promise — a launch may never happen, and a token that does launch may be worth far more or far less.
